# 黎明之剑

## 中文推荐语

网文启蒙作，永远感谢启恒的推荐。远瞳巅峰之作，最能体现网文魅力的作品，最伟大的种田类作品。势力逐渐成长，剧情环环相扣，世界观缓缓展开，行文节奏极佳，以至于633万字的体量依旧有行云流水的阅读体验。宏大的世界观，极具开创性的设定，贯穿全文的严谨大纲，严肃又不失幽默的文笔，极具人文关怀的内核...做到两个点就足以成为不错的网文，而黎明之剑在每个方面都优秀得无可比拟。游戏被称作第九艺术，而《黎明之剑》让我认识到了网文所具备的文学价值也不亚于严肃文学，甚至有很多地方是传统文学作品完全做不到，或者完全无法企及的。本书彻底改变了我对网文的认知，带我走进了全新的世界。如果我只能推荐一部网文，我一定会推荐《黎明之剑》。

## English recommendation

The book that introduced me to web fiction; I will always be grateful to Qiheng for recommending it. It is Yuan Tong at his best, a work that captures everything this form can offer, and the finest example of a story about building a society from the ground up. Its faction grows steadily, its plotlines interlock, and its world unfolds at just the right pace. The writing flows so well that even its 6.33 million Chinese characters feel effortless to read.

A vast world, strikingly original ideas, a carefully sustained narrative plan, prose that balances seriousness with humour, and a deeply humane outlook: excelling at two of these would be enough to make a good web novel. Sword of Dawnbreaker is exceptional in every one of them.

Video games have been called the ninth art. This novel showed me that web fiction, too, can have literary value equal to that of serious literature—and that in some respects, it can do things traditional literary forms cannot, or could scarcely hope to achieve. This book completely changed the way I saw web fiction and opened up a whole new world to me. If I could recommend only one web novel, it would be Sword of Dawnbreaker.

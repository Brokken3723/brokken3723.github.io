# 工业克苏鲁不相信魔法

## 中文推荐语

真冷门神作，似乎从未上过起点月票榜，很可惜现在起点不是种田题材的版本，是我最喜欢的网文中唯一一部追连载完结的。《赤色黎明》和《黎明之剑》的结合体，取二者之精华而去其糟粕。相较前者，虽然主角所代表的势力一直处于扩张状态，但主角苏文展现了极高的政治素养与国际主义精神，不搞霸权主义/帝国主义/民族主义。相较后者，本书对待神明采取了截然不同的策略，将主线与势力的外部扩张与内部斗争进行紧密的结合，并且有更鲜明具体的红色内容。本书在架空世界中推演了一场彻底的红色浪潮，除了优秀的主角描写外也有着精彩的群像刻画，叙述方式类似《赤》与《黎》，主线发展联系着分布在这个世界中的小故事推进，有引领浪潮的，有乘势而为的，有被其转变的，也有被其碾碎的。《工业克苏鲁不相信魔法》弥补了两本作品的遗憾，也走出了自己独特的道路，是我认为近几年来起点平台上最好的作品。

## English recommendation

A truly overlooked masterpiece. As far as I know, it never even appeared on Qidian's monthly-vote rankings. It is a shame that stories about building societies and developing industry have fallen out of fashion there. Of all my favourite web novels, this is the only one I followed chapter by chapter until its conclusion.

It brings together the strengths of Red Dawn and Sword of Dawnbreaker while avoiding their weaknesses. Compared with Red Dawn, although the protagonist's faction is constantly expanding, Su Wen shows remarkable political judgement and an internationalist outlook, rejecting hegemonism, imperialism, and nationalism. Compared with Sword of Dawnbreaker, it takes a very different approach to the gods, tightly weaving the main plot into the faction's outward expansion and internal struggles, with a more explicit and concrete revolutionary politics.

Within its imagined world, the novel traces a sweeping revolutionary wave. Alongside a compelling protagonist, it offers a vividly drawn ensemble. Like Red Dawn and Sword of Dawnbreaker, it advances the main story through smaller stories scattered across the world: some people lead the wave, some ride it, some are transformed by it, and others are crushed beneath it. Industrial Cthulhu Does Not Believe in Magic makes up for what I found lacking in those two works while finding its own distinctive path. To me, it is the best novel Qidian has published in recent years.

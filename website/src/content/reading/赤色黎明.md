# 赤色黎明

## 中文推荐语

红色启蒙作品（叠甲：推荐不代表认同所有作者表达的观点），或许也是能称作我人生/三观转折点的作品，再次感谢启恒的推荐。以最能让读者代入的穿越者视角，切身体会了那段伟大艰辛的历史，让曾经接触过无数次的词汇鲜活了起来，真正理解了许多事情的内核。就像本书当年对于网文圈的影响一般，让我这种成长于伤痕文学、文青电影、公知宣传的人，能够第一次实事求是地去看待那段历史。第一次体会到解放思想的感觉，继而引发了对于过往人生的彻底反思，与对未来生活方向的转变。但很可惜，作品后半段的发展并不让人满意，甚至可以说反感，现在也很难找到当年网友写的同人推演了...即便如此，《赤色黎明》也是对我影响最为深远的网文。



## English recommendation

This book introduced me to revolutionary politics and history. A caveat: recommending it does not mean endorsing every view expressed by the author. It may also mark a turning point in my life and worldview; once again, I am grateful to Qiheng for recommending it. Through the immediately relatable perspective of a protagonist transported into the past, I felt something of the greatness and hardship of that history. Terms I had encountered countless times came alive, and I began to understand what lay behind them.

Much as the novel once challenged the Chinese web-fiction community, it helped someone like me—raised on Scar Literature, art-house films, and propaganda from public intellectuals—look at that history in a grounded, fact-based way for the first time. It gave me my first sense of what it means to free one's thinking, prompting a thorough re-examination of my past life and a change in the direction I wanted to take.

Unfortunately, the second half of the novel disappointed me, at times even alienating me. It is also difficult now to find the alternative developments that readers imagined in their own fan fiction at the time. Even so, Red Dawn remains the web novel that has had the deepest influence on me.
